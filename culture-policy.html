Culture & Conduct Policy

"A great culture starts with clarity, respect, and consistency."

This document outlines the expected behavior and values to maintain a positive, inclusive, transparent, and productive work environment for everyone.

🌈 1. Respect, Inclusivity & Workplace Behavior

✅ Mutual Respect
Everyone—regardless of role or background—deserves to be treated with dignity, courtesy, and respect.
✅ Respect for Clients & Customers
Always treat our clients, customers, and end-users with professionalism, empathy, and respect. Your behavior reflects the company.
✅ Professional Language
Abusive or inappropriate language is not acceptable. Communicate respectfully and clearly.
✅ Inclusivity Matters
We are committed to fostering an inclusive space where everyone feels safe, heard, and valued.
✅ Freedom of Clothing
Employees are free to wear clothing of their choice, as long as it is comfortable, appropriate, and legal.
✅ Zero Tolerance for Discrimination
We do not tolerate hatred or discrimination based on religion, gender, race, caste, language, disability, age, sexuality, nationality or appearance.
✅ No Bullying or Harassment
Bullying, intimidation, or harassment—verbal, physical, emotional, or digital—is strictly prohibited.
✅ Respect Freedom of Speech
Speak freely and respectfully—without disrupting others or the work environment.

⚖️ 2. Ethics, Transparency & Growth Culture

✅ Whistleblowing Encouraged
Report any illegal, unethical, or harmful behavior directly to leadership or HR. Confidentiality will be maintained.
✅ Mutual Growth Philosophy
Company growth and employee growth go hand in hand. We grow stronger together.
✅ Decisions Based on Acts, Not Relationships
All decisions (roles, rewards, promotions) must be based on facts and merit, not favoritism.
✅ Company Governance
Everything we do must follow lawful, democratic, and secular principles.
✅ Suggestions to HR
We encourage you to suggest ideas for company improvement. HR will evaluate and prioritize them.
✅ Confidentiality
Protect company data and colleague privacy. Do not share sensitive information without permission.
✅ Avoid Gossip & Office Politics
Stay professional. Avoid personal discussions that may cause negativity or division.

📢 3. Communication & Conflict Resolution

📍 Report Issues Promptly
Don’t let issues build up. Report concerns to HR or your manager right away.
❓ Don’t Assume – Clarify
If something is unclear, always ask. Open communication avoids confusion and errors.
🔄 Close the Loop
Every conversation should be concluded properly with clear next steps.
📶 Priority of Communication
💬 Chat – Quick updates
📧 Email – Formal communication
🎥 Google Meet – Discussions & collaboration
📞 Call – Urgent matters
📵 Please avoid late-night messages or unnecessary calls.
💬 Constructive Feedback Culture
Appreciate publicly, correct privately.
📊 Provide Genuine Feedback
Share honest and professional input when requested.

📊 4. Work Commitments, Productivity & Discipline

📅 Task Updates
Share regular updates with your manager or task owner.
⏱️ End-of-Day Timesheet
Submit daily without fail.
🕒 Late Entry / Early Leave
Inform your manager at least one hour in advance.
🤒 Sick / Emergency Leaves
Inform 1–2 hours before.
📆 Planned Leaves
Notify at least 2 working days in advance.
📋 Weekly Work Schedule
Declare availability at start of week.
📈 Ownership & Accountability
Take responsibility and improve continuously.
⏳ Time Management & Meeting Etiquette
Join meetings on time
Use calendars
Keep discussions focused

✨ 5. Positive Culture & Team Bonding

🧘‍♂️ Work-Life Balance
Avoid overwork and respect team time.
🌟 Celebrate Small Wins
Recognize all efforts.
🧹 Clean Workspace
Maintain organized environment.
🙌 Collaboration Over Competition
Support peers and focus on team success.

🧠 6. Health, Wellness & Safety

☕ Take Breaks
Short breaks improve clarity.
💚 Mental Health
Reach out if feeling overwhelmed.
🧑‍💻 Ergonomic Workspace
Maintain proper posture and setup.
🥗 Healthy Habits
Stay hydrated
Avoid junk food
Join wellness initiatives

✅ Final Note

We expect everyone to be a custodian of our culture. Respect, communication, and integrity are the foundation of our work environment. Let’s build and protect it—together.
