Verify Certificate

Official Document & Credential Verification Portal

Welcome to the official credential authentication portal of HashGANG Solutions Private Limited. This verification gateway allows corporate recruiters, academic institutions, universities, and background verification (BGV) agencies to instantly confirm the authenticity of internship completion certificates, training records, and project experience documents issued by our organization.

How Online Verification Works

  1. Locate Document ID: Find the unique Certificate Identification Number printed at the bottom or reference header of the document (format: HRD/INT-P/XXXXXXXXX).
  2. Submit for Validation: Enter the exact alphanumeric identifier into the search field above and click Verify.
  3. Instant Telemetry Confirmation: The central database cross-references the record in real time, displaying the candidate's verified name, internship specialization, completion tenure, accredited project hours, and mode of engagement.

Document Security & Fraud Prevention Policy

Each authentic HashGANG certificate contains serialized tracking numbers and security metadata. Any document displaying altered names, modified dates, inflated training hours, or unlisted serial numbers is deemed fraudulent and legally void under applicable IT Act provisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What should I do if a certificate ID returns "Invalid Certificate"?
Ensure the Certificate ID is typed accurately, including all capital letters and slashes (e.g., HRD/INT-P/251006001). If the record was issued recently within 48 hours or during an ongoing offline batch, contact our HR team at info@hashgang.com with the candidate's full name and registered college for manual verification.

Q: Are HashGANG remote internship certificates recognized by engineering universities?
Yes. All internship programs conducted by HashGANG Solutions Private Limited follow structured learning curricula, objective project milestones, and rigorous hour logging adhering to industry academic guidelines for degree, engineering, and polytechnic requirements.

Q: Can past interns request certificate reissuance or recommendation letters?
Alumni of our engineering programs can request digital reissuance of lost credentials or employment reference letters by sending their original Certificate Number to our corporate desk.
