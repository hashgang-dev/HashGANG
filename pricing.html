Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why choose a static website over WordPress or dynamic CMS?

Static websites have no underlying database to be compromised, do not suffer from outdated plugin security breaches, and load in under 1 second. They require zero maintenance and achieve top Google Core Web Vitals scores without expensive specialized servers.

How does the 50 hours of free developer support work?

Throughout the first year, whenever you want to update text, replace photos, add new client testimonials, or adjust company details, just contact our support team. We execute the changes swiftly and debit the time against your complimentary 50 hours.

What are the renewal fees after the first year?

Starting from the second year, high-speed hosting and SSL certificate renewal is only ₹1,500/year. If you ever require additional maintenance beyond routine hosting, our pay-as-you-go developer support is available at just ₹100/hour.

Can HashGANG configure custom business email accounts?

Yes, we configure official domain-branded mailboxes (e.g., info@yourbrand.com) for only ₹100 setup for the first year, and renewal is just ₹50/year. This is a fraction of the cost of mainstream email hosting providers.

How fast can our new website be launched?

Standard 5-page static websites are typically designed, refined with your feedback, tested, and published live within 5 to 7 business days once initial content and media are shared.

Note: Domain registration costs (e.g. .com, .in) are separate and not included in hosting setup. You can connect your existing domain or we can assist you in acquiring one.
