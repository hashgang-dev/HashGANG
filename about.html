Our Story & Vision

HASHGANG SOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED is an incorporated technology enterprise and modern digital venture firm headquartered in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, India.

Guided by our philosophy "Discuss • Develop • Deploy," we design, engineer, and operate resilient software applications, cloud platforms, and decentralized real-time communication tools that serve thousands of daily users globally.

From bespoke enterprise software consulting to building high-scale consumer digital platforms, HashGANG combines modern software engineering with zero-compromise security, performance, and user privacy.

Values, Trust & Governance

We hold ourselves to rigorous standards of digital ethics, user data protection, and statutory compliance:

  • User Privacy by Design: Zero tracking for intrusive profiling. End-to-end encrypted peer-to-peer data channels for real-time media.
  • Transparent Monetization: We partner exclusively with non-adult, family-friendly advertising networks such as Google AdSense. We do not participate in predatory paywalls or sell user data.
  • Equal Opportunity & Culture: Fostering a respectful, meritocratic workplace adhering to our published Culture & Conduct Policy.
  • Statutory Compliance: Fully compliant with Section 4 & 5 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021.
