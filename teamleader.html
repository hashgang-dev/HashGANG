Team Leader

Smart Task & Performance Evolution System for Modern Organizations

Team Leader is a powerful SaaS platform that enables organizations to manage projects, assign tasks, track performance, and evaluate employee productivity—all in one place.

Core Features

📌 Task Management
📊 Performance Tracking
⏱ Timesheets
📅 Absence Logging
💬 Team Messaging
👥 Team & Project Management
📈 Employee Scoring

The Problem

Organizations often struggle with fragmented systems—tasks in one place, attendance in another, and performance reviews done manually. This leads to inefficiencies, lack of transparency, and wasted leadership time during evaluations.

Our Solution

Team Leader unifies all key workflows—tasks, timesheets, absences, and performance—into a single intelligent system. It automatically tracks activity and calculates employee performance scores, eliminating manual effort.

Key Benefits

⚡ Saves Management Time
📊 Data-Driven Appraisals
🔍 Full Transparency
📈 Boosts Productivity
