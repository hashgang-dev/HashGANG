💡 Frequently Asked Questions

How does MyLeader replace annual appraisals?

Instead of evaluating an entire year of work in a single rushed meeting, managers rate daily timesheets and completed deliverables continuously. When appraisal cycles arrive, final scores calculate automatically from this accumulated data.

Is MyLeader suitable for software and agency teams?

Yes. MyLeader is specifically engineered for software engineering, design, marketing, and professional services organizations where output is tied to project milestones, sprints, and measurable deliverables.

Can employees see their own performance scores?

Yes. Transparency is a cornerstone of MyLeader. Team members can view their scorecards, task ratings, and performance trends in real time, eliminating unpleasant surprises during review cycles.

How secure is our organizational data?

MyLeader enforces strict tenant-level isolation, JWT session validation, role-based access permissions, and encrypted AWS S3 storage. Your company data remains completely isolated and confidential.
